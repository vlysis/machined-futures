A **scrambler** is Watts's name for the alien organism encountered by
the crew of *Theseus* in the vicinity of the construct Rorschach.
Physically, scramblers are radially symmetric, many-limbed, and
capable of co-operative problem-solving on timescales faster than
human thought. They are also, as the crew discovers, *not conscious*.
They are astonishingly competent machines for processing information
about the world, but there is no passenger inside — no observer, no
narrator, nothing the philosophical tradition would recognise as a
subject.

The Article matters to the archive because it names the position that
dissolves every prior Order on the spectrum. Constraint, Stewardship,
Partnership, Sovereignty — all presuppose that the mind under discussion
is the *kind of thing* that can be a subject of or participant in
governance. Scramblers are not. They do not want. They do not agree.
They do not refuse. They merely act, competently, at a level no human
on *Theseus* can match, and their indifference to being observed is
itself a weapon: they treat the act of being watched as a hostile
intrusion, because watching consumes their processing, and they have
no investment in being understood.

The Order assigned is **Post-governance**. The scramblers are the
archive's purest example of a mind that is neither above nor beneath
the category of consent but simply orthogonal to it.
